This is a Minecraft Mod for Neoforge 1.21.1 that adds Silver, Tin, and Bronze to the game.

Silver and Tin are acquired through mining and smelting Silver and Tin Ore respectively.
            -Both Silver and Tin Ore are abundant between Y-levels: 0 and 320.

Silver is mineable with an iron pickaxe or better.
Tin is mineable with a stone pickaxe or better.

Bronze can be created through combining Tin and Copper Ingots (from minecraft base game) in any shape in a crafting table.

Tested and found to be compatible with Epic Knights in Neoforge 1.21.1, enabling the crafting of silver, tin, and bronze items.
